Body & lid

Compound even-odd ring: outer 22×24 rounded rect (r=5 → 3.4px @ 22) minus inner rect, 3-unit walls. The lid band sits at y=11–14, matching the original’s lid division at ~32% of height.

Grille bars

The original’s 15 hand-drawn bars fuse into one 6.2px band at 22px. Three 1.8-unit pill bars on the original’s exact bar pitch (700 units → 3.6) keep that rhythm without muddying the gap.

Padlock

One compound path: 3-unit shackle ring over a 5×4.5 body, centered on x=16 exactly like the source path (3735–6215 units). Reads as the accent dot at 22px, as a lock from 44px up.
